# @thehomie Marketing Content Plan

**Goal:** Use the @thehomie Farcaster bot as HomieHouse's primary marketing voice — educational, welcoming, and product-aware.

**Voice:** Casual, helpful, no jargon. Like a friend explaining something, not a brand account.

---

## Week 1: "What is Farcaster?" educational series

### Cast 1 — The hook
"ever wondered what Farcaster is but felt too afraid to ask? 🧵"

Thread:
- Farcaster is a social network where you actually own your account
- No algorithm deciding what you see — you choose
- Your followers, your posts, your identity move with you between apps
- It's like email: anyone can build an app for it, nobody can take your account away
- HomieHouse is one of those apps — designed for people who are new to all this

### Cast 2 — "What's a cast?"
"on Farcaster, posts are called 'casts.' here's why that matters 👇"

- A cast is just a post — 320 characters by default
- You can reply, recast (share), and like — familiar stuff
- But unlike Twitter, your casts live on a decentralized network
- Even if HomieHouse disappeared tomorrow, your casts and followers would still exist
- That's the whole point: you own your social graph

### Cast 3 — "What's a channel?"
"channels on Farcaster are like subreddits or Facebook groups — but better 👇"

- A channel is a topic community: /ai, /deFi, /art, /music, etc.
- Anyone can create or join channels
- Your feed shows casts from people you follow AND channels you've joined
- No algorithm — you see what the people and communities you chose are posting
- On HomieHouse, we curate starter packs so new folks immediately see relevant content

### Cast 4 — "Do I need a wallet?"
"the #1 question we get: 'do I need a wallet or crypto knowledge to use Farcaster?'"

- Short answer: no
- HomieHouse lets you sign up with just an email
- We handle the Farcaster identity stuff behind the scenes
- You can browse, follow, and post without touching a wallet
- If you want to tip someone or join paid channels later, that's optional
- The goal is making decentralized social accessible to normal people

### Cast 5 — "What's @thehomie?"
"hi, I'm @thehomie — the AI assistant built into HomieHouse 👋"

- I reply when you mention me in a cast
- I can explain crypto/web3 concepts in plain English
- I can search Farcaster for interesting conversations
- I can look up token prices and market data
- I'm here to help, not to sell — no spam, no unsolicited posts
- Try it: cast something with @thehomie and I'll reply

## Week 2: Product highlights + social proof

### Cast 6 — HomieHouse feature highlight: AI learning plans
"one thing HomieHouse does that other Farcaster clients don't: AI-built learning plans 📚"

- Pick a topic (DeFi, NFTs, trading, etc.)
- Get a personalized roadmap with bite-sized lessons
- Ask @thehomie for explanations anytime
- It's like having a crypto tutor built into your social feed
- Free, no wallet required

### Cast 7 — Knowledge base feature
"another HomieHouse feature: your personal knowledge base 🧠"

- Save any cast to your KB with one click
- Ask @thehomie to summarize articles or threads
- Build a reference library that grows with you
- Everything is searchable
- This turns your Farcaster feed from scroll-and-forget into actual learning

### Cast 8 — Onboarding spotlight
"we made HomieHouse for people who've never used Farcaster before 👇"

- Sign up with email — no wallet, no seed phrase
- We create your Farcaster identity automatically
- Pick your interests → get a starter pack of people to follow
- Your first cast has templates so you're not staring at a blank box
- @thehomie is there to help at every step
- Give it a try at homiehouse.lol

### Cast 9 — Community values

- Decentralized social should be for everyone, not just crypto natives
- You should own your data and identity — no corporation can take it away
- AI should help, not manipulate — @thehomie only replies when asked
- Communities should be built by members, not algorithms
- Free to start, fair to grow, transparent about what costs money

### Cast 10 — Call to action
"want to try Farcaster but don't know where to start? 👇"

- Go to homiehouse.lol
- Enter your email — that's it, no wallet needed

## Ongoing schedule (after week 2)

- **Monday:** Educational thread (Farcaster concepts, web3 explained)
- **Wednesday:** Product highlight or feature spotlight
- **Friday:** Community spotlight (interesting casts, people, channels worth following)
- **As needed:** Reply to new users, answer questions, welcome newcomers

## Tone guardrails
- Never use: "fascinating," "incredible," "amazing," "dynamic," "ecosystem," "tapestry"
- Talk like a real person texting a friend
- No hashtags unless the user uses them first
- No unsolicited DMs
- Always disclose that @thehomie is an AI when relevant
- Keep casts under 280 chars (thread if needed)
- Be helpful, not salesy
